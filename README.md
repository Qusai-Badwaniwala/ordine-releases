# Ordine for Android

Your tasks and prayers, kept on your phone.

[Download the latest version](https://github.com/Qusai-Badwaniwala/ordine-releases/releases/latest)

Download the Ordine APK from the release page, then open it on your Android phone and tap Install. If Ordine is already installed, keep it installed and update over it to preserve your information.

From version 0.2.2 onward, open More â†’ Updates inside Ordine to get future releases. You can turn on a daily check when opening the app. Android asks before installing an update.

Ordine works offline. Your tasks, prayer records, files and settings remain on your phone. Update checks and downloads need an internet connection. No Ordine account is needed.

This repository contains installation files and update information. It does not collect personal records.
